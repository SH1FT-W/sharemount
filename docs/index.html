ShareMount

Your network shares, always there.

A small menu bar app for the Mac that keeps SMB shares mounted and brings them back after sleep, network changes and NAS restarts.

Download View on GitHub›

Free and open source · macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon

Set it up once. Forget about it.

ShareMount watches your shares in the background and quietly fixes what macOS leaves broken.

Reconnects on its own

After wake, Wi-Fi changes and NAS reboots. While a server is away it backs off gently, from 30 seconds up to 15 minutes.

No more beachballs

Hung mounts are detected with short timeouts and remounted, but never in the first seconds after wake.

Uses the macOS keychain

Connects through macOS with the password the Finder already saved. ShareMount never reads or stores a password.

Clean paths

Keeps shares at /Volumes/Media instead of Media-1, so scripts and backups always find them.

Gentle with your NAS

A rejected password stops all automatic retries until you sign in again, so your account is never locked out.

All your Macs

With iCloud Drive, your list of shares follows you to every Mac. Passwords stay in each Mac's keychain.

Private by design.

  • No tracking

    No analytics, no telemetry, no accounts. There is nothing to sign up for.

  • Only your servers

    ShareMount talks to your own SMB servers and, to look for updates, to GitHub. Nothing else.

  • No passwords stored

    Passwords stay in the macOS keychain and are only ever used by macOS itself.

  • Plain files

    Your shares live in a readable shares.json, the log in ~/Library/Logs.

Install in a minute.

ShareMount is open source and signed locally, not notarized by Apple, so macOS asks once before the first launch.

  1. Download

    Get ShareMount.zip from the latest release and unzip it.

  2. Move to Applications

    Drag ShareMount.app into your Applications folder. Updates replace it there.

  3. Allow the first launch

    Open it, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. Or in Terminal:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/ShareMount.app

  4. Add your shares

    Add them in Settings or adopt the ones already mounted in the Finder. Sign in once with “Remember this password in my keychain”.
